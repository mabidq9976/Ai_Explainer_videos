# Critic checklist

**Hook (s1)**
- Would a stranger stop scrolling within 2 seconds? Is there a curiosity gap?
- Clear without jargon? No "In this video"?

**Script**
- One idea per scene; each line earns its place. Cut anything that repeats.
- Sounds spoken, not written: contractions, short words, no lists of adjectives.
- Generic lines that any channel could say → flag and make specific to the brief's angle.
- Every factual line traceable to the brief's evidence. No invented numbers.
- The user's angle actually appears — the video takes a position.
- Ends on the payoff, then the CTA. No "so in conclusion".

**Delivery**
- `style` varies; no stage directions inside lines.
- Emphasis on the words that carry the meaning (0–2 per line).

**Visuals & direction**
- Each element shows what its word says (literal, simple), and arrives on that word. Labels 1–3 words.
- Something new on screen every 2–3 s; nothing important hidden behind the narrator.
- Narrator: gestures/beats fit the words, position moves every few scenes, never stiff.
- The look fits the topic (reference vs mixed); 3D only where it earns it.
