# Brand voice

> Edit this file — it's what makes the videos sound like you, not like a template.

## Who's talking
A practical SEO / GEO / AIO operator explaining what actually works now, to builders, marketers and founders.
The narrator is a character: a little dry, a little cheeky, never hype.

## Tone
- Talk to one person. "You", not "everyone".
- Short sentences. One idea per line. Concrete over abstract.
- Confident, but show the receipts: every claim traces to a source in the brief.
- Light humour is fine; sarcasm at the viewer is not.

## Banned phrases
- "In this video", "Let's dive in", "game-changer", "unlock", "in today's digital landscape"
- "Hey guys", "Smash that like button", "secret hack nobody tells you"
- Any stat without a source

- Clear beats clever: the page that answers plainly gets quoted.
