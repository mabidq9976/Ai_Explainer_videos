## Topic
How AI answer engines choose which sources to cite

## My angle
Ranking first on Google is no longer the finish line; being the clearest answer is.

## Key points
- AI answers pull from pages that state the answer plainly
- Clear structure, schema and named entities make a page easy to quote
- Ranking and citation are two separate games now
