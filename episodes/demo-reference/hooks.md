## Winner
**Why does AI quote your competitor and not you?** (question)
It opens on a pain the viewer already feels, it makes no claim that needs a source, and it sets up the angle as the answer.

## Alternate
**You don't need more content. You need content AI can quote.** (bold-claim)
This is the brief's angle, word for word. If the question wins, use this as the second line.
